Privacy Policy

NoPing Monitor and NoPing Monitor Pro · Last updated: November 2026

English

NoPing Monitor does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. Everything the app shows is read from your Mac and stays on your Mac.

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Your preferences and the result of the disk analysis are stored only on your Mac.
  • NoPing Monitor (Mac App Store) does not connect to the internet.
  • NoPing Monitor Pro sends your license key and the name of your Mac to Lemon Squeezy, the store that sells the license, only to confirm the purchase. To check for updates, it reads the update list published on this website.

Questions: suporte@noping.com · 1ncrivel Sistemas LTDA

Português
